How My SIP Fulfills These Objectives

The Irrigation Well Control System demonstrates achievement across all six objectives:

Objective 1: Design Robotic & Embedded Systems

My system combines Arduino microcontrollers with LoRa modules to create an embedded solution that addresses a real-world irrigation control challenge.

Objective 2: Embedded Microprocessor & Circuit Skills

Demonstrates embedded microprocessor and circuit skills through Arduino control logic, relay circuits, power management, and LoRa communication hardware.

Objective 3: Mechanical Control Systems

Integrates transducers, actuators, feedback, sensing, and relay-based control into a mechanical irrigation platform for reliable physical actuation.

Objective 4: Robotics Applications

Examines how robotics and embedded control can be applied to agriculture, remote equipment operation, safety improvement, and real-world automation.

Objective 5: Hardware & Software Integration

Models, analyzes, and designs a system that integrates software logic with hardware components to control an autonomous mechanical process.

Objective 6: AI & Data Systems

Creates a foundation for adding artificial intelligence and data systems, such as sensor data tracking, predictive maintenance, and automated decision-making.
