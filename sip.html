Senior Innovation Project

Remote Irrigation Well Control System

Project Description

Irrigation wells do not have effective remote shutdown switches. Most do not have any remote switches because existing solutions are unreliable and expensive. This creates dangerous situations where operators must physically travel to wells in hazardous weather conditions.

The Problem

  • Not Reliable: Existing systems frequently fail
  • Expensive: Current commercial solutions are cost-prohibitive
  • Dangerous: Manual shutdown requires physical presence at the well, even in storms

Innovation Claim

🔧 LoRa-Based Remote Shutdown System

A simple, affordable, and reliable Arduino connection at the well paired with LoRa radio modules to safely ground or disconnect the well from your home.

Why It's Innovative

  • Much cheaper than existing commercial solutions
  • Works with gas or electric wells
  • More reliable than current alternatives
  • No subscription fees or cloud dependency
  • Off-grid operation with low power consumption

Key Advantages

  • 10-15 mile range with high-gain antennas
  • Sub-GHz frequency penetrates obstacles
  • Totally off-grid, no internet required
  • Battery-powered operation (months per charge)
  • No moving parts = fewer failure points

How It Works

LoRa Modules
Transmit on sub-GHz frequencies (915 MHz in US)
Arduino Receiver
Receives signal and triggers relay module
Relay Module
Acts as physical switch to cut power/signal
Remote Control
Operator controls from home or safe location
