Robotics & Embedded Systems — Boards Portfolio

Boards is a separate graduation portfolio from my Student Innovation Project (SIP). Each of the six program objectives requires evidence from two distinct projects. A single project may support no more than two objectives. The assignments below are proposed evidence selections, not yet faculty-approved completions.

Evidence and Next Steps

Completed coursework provides a foundation for Boards, but the objective-specific evidence and faculty review remain in progress.

Completed coursework

Robotic arm, seven-segment display, motor control, potentiometer/photoresistor, sensor, and Simon game labs. Collect original code, schematics, lab reports, and screenshots.

Well Killer SIP

Bench-tested LoRa messaging, Nextion control, relay pulse, and simulated optocoupler feedback. Actual Murphy/engine shutdown has not been validated. Use this project for Objectives 1 and 4 only.

Objective 4

Add explicit evaluation of use cases, design alternatives, limitations, and tradeoffs for the SIP and sensor lab.

Objective 5 — evidence gap

The Simon and motor-control labs are candidates, not confirmed demonstrations of autonomy. Identify or implement autonomous mechanical behavior before claiming this objective is met.

Objective 6 — planned

Assignments 4.1 (voice) and 4.2 (vision) are due November 23. Integrate AI output with a robotic platform or simulation and document tests. They are not complete yet.

Revision deadline

Target initial evidence assembly by November 23 and complete revisions by approximately November 26, 2026.
