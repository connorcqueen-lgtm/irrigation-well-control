How My SIP Fulfills These Objectives

The Irrigation Well Control System demonstrates achievement across all six objectives:

Objective 1: Design IoT Systems

My system combines Arduino microcontrollers with LoRa modules to create a complete IoT solution that connects remote well sites with home-based control stations.

Objective 2: Network Communication

Implements sub-GHz LoRa communication protocols for secure, encrypted remote command transmission with 10-15 mile range.

Objective 3: Mechanical Design

Integrates relay modules and mechanical switches with electronic control systems for reliable physical actuation.

Objective 4: System Performance

Designed for ultra-low power consumption with multi-month battery life and costs under $300 - 10x cheaper than commercial alternatives.

Objective 5: Scalable Solutions

Architecture supports multiple wells and receiver stations without requiring expensive cloud services or monthly subscriptions.

Objective 6: Safety & Reliability

Eliminates dangerous manual shutdown processes, reduces single points of failure, and uses time-tested LoRa technology for remote operations.
