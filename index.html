About Me

I'm an IoT and Robotics engineer passionate about creating innovative solutions to real-world problems. My work focuses on affordable, reliable systems that make a tangible impact.

My background includes:

  • LoRa & IoT Networking
  • 3D Design & Robotics
  • Embedded Systems
  • Network Security & Architecture
