Remote Irrigation Well Shutdown System

An innovative, affordable, and reliable LoRa-based remote control system for irrigation wells

Explore Solution Learn More

The Problem

Irrigation wells do not have effective remote shutdown switches. Most do not have any remote switches because the ones that are available are:

  • Not Reliable - Existing systems frequently fail
  • Expensive - Current solutions are cost-prohibitive
  • Dangerous - Manual shutdown requires physical presence at the well, even in hazardous weather

This creates unnecessary risk and operational challenges for farmers and property managers.

⚠️

LoRa Remote Shutdown System

A Simple, Innovative Solution

Arduino + LoRa Modules

Overview: A simple Arduino connection at the well paired with LoRa radio modules to safely ground or disconnect the well from your home.

Key Advantages:

  • ✅ Much cheaper than existing commercial solutions
  • ✅ Works with gas or electric wells
  • ✅ More reliable than current alternatives
  • ✅ No subscription fees or cloud dependency
  • ✅ Off-grid operation with low power consumption

How It Works:

  1. LoRa Modules transmit on sub-GHz frequencies (915 MHz in US)
  2. Signal easily penetrates obstacles with massive range on very low power
  3. Arduino receives command and triggers relay module output
  4. Relay acts as physical switch to cut power/signal to target device

Technical Details:

Hardware:
HopeRF RFM95W or pre-built boards (Adafruit LoRa Feather, Heltec LoRa V3)
Range:
10–15 miles Line-of-Sight with high-gain antennas
Power:
Very low power consumption
Complexity:
Moderate

Why Choose LoRa?

Sub-GHz Frequency

915 MHz (US) easily penetrates obstacles like buildings, trees, and terrain without degradation

Massive Range

10-15 miles of coverage is highly achievable with proper antenna placement and line-of-sight

Zero Subscriptions

No monthly fees, no cloud service required - just your own hardware

Emergency Reliable

Works when power is out and during emergencies - doesn't depend on cellular or internet
